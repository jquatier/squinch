# @squinch/pack-gcp

Google Cloud's product icons (the May 2026 library, 45 icons) for
[Squinch](https://squinch.cc/). Squinch installs this pack for you: draw an icon
as `gcp/<id>`, and `squinch icons search --pack gcp <term>` finds one. Most
products draw with their category's icon, as Google intends.
